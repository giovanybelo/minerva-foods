CAMERA: ARRI Alexa 35 at ISO 800, at knee height, three-quarter front, strong Dutch tilt, the runner leaping toward and past the lens.

LENS: Canon K35 24mm T1.5 at T5.6, extreme wide-angle, focus on the leading shoe mid-air.

LIGHT: Hard noon sun at 5.600K high camera-right, crisp starburst on the frame edge. No fill. Deep cobalt sky, sunlit shoe at 90 IRE, splash droplets glinting.

SUBJECT: A runner in Campinas, Brazil, airborne over a puddle on the asphalt, leading trail running shoe thrust toward the lens, water splash frozen around the outsole. Sunglasses, headband. A small gold medallion on a floral ribbon flies up off his chest, secondary, seared grill-mark texture, no letters.

FOREGROUND: The leading shoe huge and razor sharp, splash droplets frozen.

MIDGROUND: Body stretched in the leap, arms wide, medallion mid-bounce.

BACKGROUND: Tilted Campinas skyline with towers and ipê trees, deep blue sky.

WARDROBE TONAL BEHAVIOR: Plain cobalt vest, orange shorts, white crew socks, no logos, saturated under hard sun.

MAKEUP SURFACE PHYSICS: Real sweat and splash water beading on skin, veins visible.

POST BEHAVIOR: 35mm film grain structure, light visible grain, punchy saturation, crisp micro-contrast, deep cobalt sky.

COMPOSITIONAL GEOMETRY: Tilted horizon, shoe breaking the lower-right corner, body diagonal to upper-left, sky as negative space.

MOOD & ART DIRECTION: Composition and art direction inspired in the work of award-winning directors.
