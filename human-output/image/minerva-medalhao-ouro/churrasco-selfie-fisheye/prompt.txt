CAMERA: ARRI Alexa 35 at ISO 800, hand-held at arm's length, slightly above the couple, strong Dutch tilt, inches from their faces.

LENS: Canon K35 24mm T1.5 at T4, extreme wide-angle barrel distortion, focus on faces and food.

LIGHT: Hard morning sun at 5.600K high camera-left, raking across cheeks. No fill. Skin at 80 IRE, jaw shadows at 20 IRE, glints on the meat.

SUBJECT: Two young runners, a man and a woman, heads pressed together, leaning into the lens, each biting a slice of grilled picanha off a metal skewer, lips puckered. She holds a small wooden board of sliced medium-rare picanha close to the lens. Both wear wraparound sport sunglasses with orange mirrored lenses reflecting the photographer.

FOREGROUND: Sliced picanha, pink center, charred fat cap, glistening juices, soft from distortion.

MIDGROUND: Faces cheek to cheek, skewers at their mouths, sharp.

BACKGROUND: Sunlit road curving away, dry hillside, deep blue sky tilted.

WARDROBE TONAL BEHAVIOR: Plain black running tees, no logos or text, matte fabric.

MAKEUP SURFACE PHYSICS: Real skin, post-run sweat, stubble, sun-flushed cheeks, grease on fingers.

POST BEHAVIOR: 35mm film grain structure, visible organic grain, warm teal-orange balance, punchy saturation, lens vignette.
