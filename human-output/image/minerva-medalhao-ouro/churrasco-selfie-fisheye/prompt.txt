CAMERA: ARRI Alexa 35 at ISO 800, hand-held at arm's length, slightly above the couple, strong Dutch tilt, inches from their faces.

LENS: Canon K35 24mm T1.5 at T4, extreme wide-angle barrel distortion, focus on faces and skewers.

LIGHT: Hard morning sun at 5.600K high camera-left, dappled by leaves. No fill. Skin at 80 IRE, jaw shadows at 20 IRE, glints on the meat.

SUBJECT: Two young runners, a man and a woman, heads pressed together, leaning into the lens, each biting chunks of grilled beef off a rustic wooden skewer, mid-bite. Wraparound sport sunglasses with orange mirrored lenses reflecting the photographer. Nothing else in their hands.

FOREGROUND: Wooden skewers with charred beef chunks, glistening, soft from distortion.

MIDGROUND: Faces cheek to cheek, meat at their mouths, sharp.

BACKGROUND: Ibirapuera Park in São Paulo: a tree-lined running path, the lake, the white modernist dome and curving concrete canopy, city towers beyond, blue sky.

WARDROBE TONAL BEHAVIOR: Plain black running tees, no logos or text, matte fabric.

MAKEUP SURFACE PHYSICS: Real skin, post-run sweat, sun-flushed cheeks, grease on fingers.

POST BEHAVIOR: 35mm film grain structure, visible organic grain, warm teal-orange balance, punchy saturation, lens vignette.

COMPOSITIONAL GEOMETRY: Faces filling the frame, skewers crossing the lower third, tilted horizon as diagonal tension.

MOOD & ART DIRECTION: Composition and art direction inspired in the work of award-winning directors.
