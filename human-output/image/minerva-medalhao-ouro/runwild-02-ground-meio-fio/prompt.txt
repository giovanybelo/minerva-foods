CAMERA: ARRI Alexa 35 at ISO 800, set on the curb at ground level, lens inches from the pavement, side-on to the runner's path.

LENS: Canon K35 24mm T1.5 at T5.6, extreme wide-angle, focus on the shoe at the moment of impact.

LIGHT: Hard midday sun at 5.600K raking from camera-left, sharp shadow under the foot. No fill. Deep cobalt sky, sunlit shoe at 90 IRE, dust grains glinting.

SUBJECT: A runner in Campinas, Brazil, his trail running shoe striking the asphalt right beside the lens, dust puffing off the outsole, the rest of his body rising tall above. A small gold medallion on a floral ribbon bounces at his chest, soft and secondary, seared grill-mark texture, no letters. Sunglasses, headband.

FOREGROUND: The shoe huge and razor sharp, laces, mesh and scuffed rubber in detail.

MIDGROUND: Calf and knee in motion, the runner's torso foreshortened above.

BACKGROUND: Campinas street with flowering ipê trees and mid-rise towers, deep blue sky.

WARDROBE TONAL BEHAVIOR: Plain cobalt vest, orange shorts, white crew socks, no logos, saturated under hard sun.

MAKEUP SURFACE PHYSICS: Real sweat on the calf, dust clinging to the sock.

POST BEHAVIOR: 35mm film grain structure, light visible grain, punchy saturation, crisp micro-contrast, warm skin.

COMPOSITIONAL GEOMETRY: Shoe anchoring the lower-left third, runner rising diagonally to the upper right, tilted horizon.

MOOD & ART DIRECTION: Composition and art direction inspired in the work of award-winning directors.
