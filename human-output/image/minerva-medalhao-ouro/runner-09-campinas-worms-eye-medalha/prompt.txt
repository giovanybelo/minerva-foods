CAMERA: ARRI Alexa 35 at ISO 800, on the asphalt, extreme worm's-eye angle as the runner strides over the lens.

LENS: Canon K35 24mm T1.5 at T4, focus on chest and face, near leg stretched by wide-angle distortion.

LIGHT: Morning sun at 5.600K behind the runner's shoulder, starburst flare at frame top. Zero fill, only bounce off pale pavement. Sky at 60 IRE, shoulders at 90 IRE, droplets catching glints.

SUBJECT: A runner in the streets of Campinas, Brazil, striding over the camera, squeezing a clear unlabeled bottle so water sprays into the sun. A large gold medallion on a colorful floral ribbon hangs at his neck, slightly soft, its surface textured like seared beef with crosshatched grill marks, no letters.

FOREGROUND: Leading knee soft with motion in the lower third, foot small and out of focus.

MIDGROUND: Torso, medallion swinging off-axis.

BACKGROUND: Campinas mid-rise towers, the neo-gothic cathedral spire, flowering ipê trees, deep blue sky.

WARDROBE TONAL BEHAVIOR: Plain cobalt singlet and orange shorts, sweat-darkened, no logos.

MAKEUP SURFACE PHYSICS: Real skin, sweat and water beading on arms and quads, pores visible.

POST BEHAVIOR: 35mm film grain structure, visible coarse grain, saturated blues, warm skin, halation on the flare.

COMPOSITIONAL GEOMETRY: Runner rising diagonally lower-left to upper-right, flare anchoring top-left, sky as negative space.

MOOD & ART DIRECTION: Composition and art direction inspired in the work of award-winning directors.
