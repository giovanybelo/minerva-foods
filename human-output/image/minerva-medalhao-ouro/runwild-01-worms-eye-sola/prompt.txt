CAMERA: ARRI Alexa 35 at ISO 800, lying on the asphalt, extreme worm's-eye angle, the runner stepping directly over the lens.

LENS: Canon K35 24mm T1.5 at T5.6, deep focus, extreme wide-angle stretch, the shoe sole looming huge and sharp in the foreground.

LIGHT: Hard noon sun at 5.600K behind the runner's raised hand, crisp starburst flare. No fill. Deep cobalt sky, sunlit skin at 88 IRE, water spray glittering.

SUBJECT: A runner in Campinas, Brazil, mid-stride over the camera, dusty trail shoe sole-first toward the lens, lugs in crisp detail. He squeezes a clear unlabeled bottle, water arcing into the sun. Sunglasses, headband. A small gold medallion on a floral ribbon hangs at his chest, secondary, seared grill-mark texture, no letters.

FOREGROUND: The shoe sole filling the lower third, razor sharp.

MIDGROUND: Muscular leg and torso rising diagonally, medallion swinging.
