CAMERA: ARRI Alexa 35 at ISO 800, hand-held at chest level, low oblique angle, tracking alongside the group at running pace.

LENS: Canon K35 35mm T1.5, wide open, focus on the lead runner's face and driving shoulder, the pack behind dissolving into blur.

LIGHT: Single hard source — midday sun at 5.500K, low camera-right, skimming across the runners' faces and collarbones. Zero fill. Lit cheek at 70 IRE, shadow side falling to 22 IRE, edges razor sharp.

SUBJECT: A small group of runners crossing an urban street mid-stride, the lead runner's jaw set and arm driving forward past the lens, two more runners intercepted at the edges of frame, caught at different points of the gait cycle. No footwear or ground in frame, no visible logos or brand marks on the clothing.

FOREGROUND: A blurred shoulder of a trailing runner passing close to the lens, out of focus, slicing one edge of the frame.

MIDGROUND: The lead runner's face and chest, sharply resolved at the peak of exertion.

BACKGROUND: Streaked urban color — parked cars, a crosswalk, storefronts — motion-compressed into soft horizontal bands, no readable signage.

WARDROBE TONAL BEHAVIOR: Plain unbranded technical fabric in muted single tones across the group, clinging with sweat, no visible marks.
