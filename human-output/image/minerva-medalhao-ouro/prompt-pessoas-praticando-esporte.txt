CAMERA: ARRI Alexa 35 at ISO 800, hand-held at chest level, low oblique angle, tracking alongside the athlete at pace.

LENS: Canon K35 35mm T1.5, wide open, focus on the athlete's face and driving shoulder, everything past mid-body dissolving into blur.

FOREGROUND: A blurred shoulder or limb of another person passing close to the lens, out of focus, slicing one edge of the frame.

BACKGROUND: Streaked urban or open-air color, motion-compressed into soft horizontal bands, no readable signage.
