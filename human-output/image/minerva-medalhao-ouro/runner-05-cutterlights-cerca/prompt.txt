CAMERA: ARRI Alexa 35 at ISO 800, locked on a tripod, slight low angle, lens axis 15 degrees below eye line.

LENS: Canon K35 55mm T1.5, T2, focus on the lit eye, falling on the shadow side.

LIGHT: Single hard source at 5.200K, 65 degrees camera-right, cut by a chain-link fence positioned a few feet from the runner, casting a clean grid of shadow bars across the torso and the wall behind. Lit bands at 68 IRE, shadow bands at 14 IRE, transition sharp.

SUBJECT: A runner paused mid-stretch against the fence, chest and shoulders sliced by graphic shadow lines, gaze direct into the camera. Intercepted, only the torso and shoulders lit; the rest absorbed into shadow bands. No visible logos or brand marks on the clothing.

FOREGROUND: Plain concrete surface, one diagonal band of light raking across.

MIDGROUND: Runner's torso sliced by shadow bars, geometric and graphic.

BACKGROUND: Plain wall, the same shadow bars repeating, architectural and rigid.

WARDROBE TONAL BEHAVIOR: Plain unbranded dark technical fabric, only the collar and one shoulder catching a band of light.

MAKEUP SURFACE PHYSICS: Real skin, sweat in the lit band, no powder, eyes wet and reflective.

POST BEHAVIOR: Kodak Double-X 5222 monochrome emulation, heavy visible grain, deep blacks, sharp highlight roll-off, no halation, maximum graphic contrast.

COMPOSITIONAL GEOMETRY: Horizontal bands as graphic ruling, subject sliced into thirds by the bars, architectural rigor.

MOOD & ART DIRECTION: Composition and art direction inspired in the work of award-winning directors.
