CAMERA: ARRI Alexa 35 at ISO 800, hand-held at chest level, low oblique angle, tracking alongside the runner at pace.

LENS: Canon K35 55mm T1.5, T2, focus locked on the runner's face and driving arm, background dissolving into streaks.

LIGHT: Single hard source — late-morning sun at 5.400K, low camera-right, skimming across the runner's face and shoulders. Zero fill. Lit cheek at 68 IRE, shadow side falling to 24 IRE.

SUBJECT: Runner mid-stride, jaw set, one arm driving forward past the lens, torso leaning into the effort. Intercepted by the blurred shoulder of another runner passing close in the foreground. No footwear or ground visible in frame — cropped tight from mid-thigh up.

FOREGROUND: Blurred shoulder and arm of a second runner, out of focus, slicing the left edge.

MIDGROUND: The runner's face and chest, sharply resolved at the point of maximum effort.

BACKGROUND: Streaked urban color, motion-compressed into horizontal bands.

WARDROBE TONAL BEHAVIOR: Plain unbranded technical singlet in faded terracotta, no visible marks or logos, fabric clinging with sweat.

MAKEUP SURFACE PHYSICS: Real skin, sweat streaking the temple and jaw, visible pores, no retouching artifacts.

POST BEHAVIOR: 35mm film grain structure, heavy tactile grain, hard highlight roll-off, restrained saturation, motion streaking embedded in the exposure.

COMPOSITIONAL GEOMETRY: Runner pushed to frame-right third, motion blur as the dominant graphic vector, tight intercepted crop.

MOOD & ART DIRECTION: Composition and art direction inspired in the work of award-winning directors.
