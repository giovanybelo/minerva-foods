CAMERA: ARRI Alexa 35 at ISO 800, at ankle height directly behind the runner, tilted up, trailing him down a sloping city street.

LENS: Canon K35 24mm T1.5 at T5.6, extreme wide-angle, focus on the heel of the rear shoe kicking back toward the lens.

LIGHT: Hard morning sun at 5.600K ahead of the runner, low starburst flare between his legs. No fill. Rim light outlining calves and shoulders, sky at 65 IRE.

SUBJECT: A runner in Campinas, Brazil, seen from behind, rear trail running shoe kicked up toward the lens, sole and heel counter in crisp detail, the other foot pushing off. Over his shoulder, a floral ribbon and the edge of a small gold medallion catch the sun, secondary, no letters.

FOREGROUND: The rear shoe sole huge and sharp, grit falling from the lugs.

MIDGROUND: Muscular calves and back, sweat glinting in rim light.

BACKGROUND: Campinas street descending toward towers and the cathedral spire, ipê trees, deep blue sky.

WARDROBE TONAL BEHAVIOR: Plain cobalt vest, orange shorts, white crew socks, no logos, rim-lit edges.

MAKEUP SURFACE PHYSICS: Real sweat on the back of the neck and calves.

POST BEHAVIOR: 35mm film grain structure, light visible grain, punchy saturation, crisp micro-contrast, warm flare halation.

COMPOSITIONAL GEOMETRY: Shoe bottom-center, legs forming a V framing the flare, street lines converging to the horizon.

MOOD & ART DIRECTION: Composition and art direction inspired in the work of award-winning directors.
